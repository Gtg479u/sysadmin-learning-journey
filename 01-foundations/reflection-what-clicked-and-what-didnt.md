# Reflection: What Clicked and What Didn’t (Active Directory Foundations)

## Getting Started
A lot of this was new for me, especially trying to wrap my head around configuring virtual machines. At work, you hear so many words and phrases thrown around by more senior IT folks, and you want to be on the same page. But honestly, a lot of it is just gibberish until you actually sit down and try to learn it for yourself.

## Why I Started This Project
The main reason I started this project is to gain real, hands-on experience with system administration skills so that when an opportunity comes up, I’m actually ready for it. I’m motivated to learn and I want to show initiative instead of waiting to be taught or waiting for the “perfect” moment. This lab is my way of building confidence, experience, and proof that I’m actively working toward that next step.

## What Confused Me
What confused me the most at first was the terminology and the “why” behind certain VM configurations. I could follow steps, but I didn’t always understand why things were set up a certain way or how different pieces were connected.

## What Frustrated Me
The most frustrating part was when the domain controller wouldn’t connect with GT01 and I couldn’t figure out why. I spent a lot of time troubleshooting and going in circles, only to eventually realize it was an IP address and DNS-related issue. That was both frustrating and eye-opening.

## What Finally Clicked
One of the biggest takeaways for me is that patience really matters. I can’t rush my learning just because I want to be at a certain level already. This stuff takes time, repetition, and actually breaking things before it starts to make sense.

## Looking Ahead
I’m glad to be on this journey and I’m hoping I can retain what I’ve learned and start making stronger connections to my current role. The goal is to keep building on this foundation and slowly become more confident, both technically and in conversations with others on the team.
